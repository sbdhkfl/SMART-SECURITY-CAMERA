# Privacy

This project is designed to keep biometric data and recordings on the user's own hardware.

Depending on configuration, the system may store:
- enrolled face samples or embeddings
- unknown-person snapshots
- video event clips
- microphone recordings
- event timestamps and recognition results
- system logs

The implementation will provide enrollment, listing, deletion, retention, and backup/restore controls.

Before recording or recognizing people, check the laws and rules applicable to the location and use case. Give appropriate notice and obtain consent where required.
